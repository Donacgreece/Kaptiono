FAQ

The essentials, without the fine print.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. The original video stays on your device. In Local mode the audio stays local too. In Cloud High Accuracy, only the audio required for transcription is sent.

Is Kaptiono free?

The core Local workflow remains free and exports do not carry a watermark.

Do I need an account?

Not for the core workflow. You can open Kaptiono and start with a video without mandatory sign-up.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Kaptiono is designed for mobile and desktop, with a PWA install flow and dedicated attention to Safari/iPhone compatibility.

What can I export?

You can download SRT, transcript and video with burned-in captions. Social Compatible mode is available for better social-platform compatibility.

Which video formats are supported?

The core workflow supports MP4, MOV, M4V and WebM, with a compatibility fallback for difficult Safari audio cases.
