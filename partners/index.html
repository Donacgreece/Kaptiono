OUR PRINCIPLES

Revenue should never break the product.

01

No deceptive advertising

We do not accept placements that imitate download buttons, system messages or editor controls.

02

Do not interrupt the creator

We avoid intrusive popups or forced interruptions while a user is transcribing, editing or exporting.

03

Privacy-first partnerships

Partners do not get access to user video, audio, captions or transcripts. Measurement must respect consent and privacy.

04

Clear disclosure

Sponsored partnerships are clearly labeled. Users should always know when they are seeing commercial content.
